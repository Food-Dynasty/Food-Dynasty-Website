Our Story

Food Dynasty Supermarket is a family-owned business on Bay Street, dedicated to quality, freshness and real value for the families of Staten Island.

We're more than a supermarket. Stop in for your weekly groceries, grab a hot meal from our buffet, or let us cater your next event. Everything you need is under one roof.

We're grateful to keep growing with the community we call home, and we look forward to serving you for many years to come.
