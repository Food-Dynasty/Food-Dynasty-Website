Party trays, giant heroes and full buffet packages for birthdays, office parties, holidays and family gatherings.

How to Order

  1. Pick your food from the menu below.
  2. Send your order online, call us at 718-816-1100, or stop by the deli counter. We'll confirm, and a 50% deposit holds your order.
  3. Pick up your order at 1071 Bay St, or get it delivered for $15, ready to heat and serve.

Need to change or cancel? Let us know at least 3 days before pickup. See all ordering policies.

Upgrades, heating instructions & deposits

Upgrades: fish $2 more per person. Filet mignon, skirt steak or short ribs $3 more per person. Ask about gluten-free options.

Heating: all hot trays come fully cooked. Preheat the oven to 350°F and heat for 30 minutes. If using chafing racks, heat for 2 hours.

Deposits: racks and water pans require a deposit, fully refunded when returned within 1 week.

Smaller breakfast? Our Continental Breakfast (muffins, danishes, bagels, croissants, fresh fruit, coffee, tea and orange juice) is $10 per person, minimum 20, paper goods included.
